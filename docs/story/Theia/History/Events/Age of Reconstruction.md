# Age of Reconstruction

This note covers postwar recovery and alliance formation. The overlapping [[Age of Silence and Anchor Network Development]] follows the Conclave's reconstruction of Minor Resonators, Regional Anchors and early Major Anchors. Their later cumulative success is covered in [[Network Threshold and Modern Awakening]], before the player campaign begins.
