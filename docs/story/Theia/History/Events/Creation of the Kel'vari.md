# Creation of the Kel'vari

The [[Kel'vari]] are created solely by [[The Ascendant]] after the six original species in [[Mortal Creation]]. [[Awakening of the Seven]] begins before mortal creation. Nhal has no role in the Kel'vari's creation; its first influence comes only with the later [[First Resonance]].
