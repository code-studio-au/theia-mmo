# Creation of the Kel'vari

The [Kel'vari](../../Lore/Peoples/Kel%27vari.md) are created solely by [The Ascendant](../../Characters/Divine/The%20Ascendant.md) after the six original species in [Mortal Creation](Mortal%20Creation.md). [Awakening of the Seven](Awakening%20of%20the%20Seven.md) begins before mortal creation. Nhal has no role in the Kel'vari's creation; its first influence comes only with the later [First Resonance](First%20Resonance.md).
