# Age of Silence and Anchor Network Development

After [The Binding](The%20Binding.md), Nhal's reliable communication ceases. Corruption around the original First Anchor persists, but fresh instructions do not guide the [Corrupted Conclave](../../Lore/Institutions/The%20Corrupted%20Conclave.md)'s reconstruction programme. This long era overlaps the [Age of Reconstruction](Age%20of%20Reconstruction.md) and later history; its position in the timeline does not mean the silence begins only after reconstruction ends.

## Development before the player arrives

1. **Minor Resonators:** [Thes'var](../../Characters/Thes%27var.md) reconstructs fragmentary six-field workings. Some resonate faintly with the existing Veil breach from distant sites, producing local disturbances without restoring Nhal's voice.
2. **Regional Anchors:** successive generations develop more stable linked installations. Their cumulative effects redistribute access to existing primordial energy and place a limited strain on the Shroud, still without restoring communication.
3. **Early Major Anchors:** the Conclave attempts larger workings to coordinate Regional Anchors. Some remain incomplete or dormant; no single installation can restore the connection alone.

[Su'vareth](../../Characters/Su%27vareth.md) obtains materials and protects contacts; [Khor'vun](../../Characters/Khor%27vun.md) secures sites; [Oma'vira](../../Characters/Oma%27vira.md) sustains devotion; [Vaer'uun](../../Characters/Vaer%27uun.md) preserves the authority of Nhal's remembered purpose. The network is developed over generations before the present-day player campaign.

[Network Threshold and Modern Awakening](Network%20Threshold%20and%20Modern%20Awakening.md) covers the later cumulative success. [Shattered Delegation](Shattered%20Delegation.md) is a separate Unmade operation, not the network's founding event. Exact dates and durations are not assigned here.
