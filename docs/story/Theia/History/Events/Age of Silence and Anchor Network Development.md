# Age of Silence and Anchor Network Development

After [[The Binding]], [[Nhal]]'s reliable communication ceases. Corruption around the original First Anchor persists, but fresh instructions do not guide the [[The Corrupted Conclave|Corrupted Conclave]]'s reconstruction programme. This long era overlaps the [[Age of Reconstruction]] and later history; its position in the timeline does not mean the silence begins only after reconstruction ends.

## Development before the player arrives

1. **Minor Resonators:** [[Thes'var]] reconstructs fragmentary six-field workings. Some resonate faintly with the existing Veil breach from distant sites, producing local disturbances without restoring Nhal's voice.
2. **Regional Anchors:** successive generations develop more stable linked installations. Their cumulative effects redistribute access to existing primordial energy and place a limited strain on the Shroud, still without restoring communication.
3. **Early Major Anchors:** the Conclave attempts larger workings to coordinate Regional Anchors. Some remain incomplete or dormant; no single installation can restore the connection alone.

[[Su'vareth]] obtains materials and protects contacts; [[Khor'vun]] secures sites; [[Oma'vira]] sustains devotion; [[Vaer'uun]] preserves the authority of Nhal's remembered purpose. The network is developed over generations before the present-day player campaign.

[[Network Threshold and Modern Awakening]] covers the later cumulative success. [[Shattered Delegation]] is a separate Unmade operation, not the network's founding event. Exact dates and durations are not assigned here.
