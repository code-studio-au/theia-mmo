# Mortal Creation

The six original mortal species arise after divine awakening: elves, orcs, dwarves, humans, halflings, then trolls. Ascendant joins the other gods after the first five species exist and co-creates trolls with Keeper. [[Creation of the Kel'vari]] follows: the Kel'vari are a later mortal people, distinct from [[Awakening of the Seven]].
