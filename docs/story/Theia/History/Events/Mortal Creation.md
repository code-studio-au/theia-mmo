# Mortal Creation

The six original mortal species arise after divine awakening: elves, orcs, dwarves, humans, halflings, then trolls. Ascendant joins the other gods after the first five species exist and co-creates trolls with Keeper. [Creation of the Kel'vari](Creation%20of%20the%20Kel%27vari.md) follows: the Kel'vari are a later mortal people, distinct from [Awakening of the Seven](Awakening%20of%20the%20Seven.md).
