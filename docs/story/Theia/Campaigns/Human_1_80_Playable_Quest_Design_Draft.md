# Theia — Human 1–80 Playable Main-Quest Design Draft

**Status: Needs Review — 2 November 2026.** New quest objectives are proposals, not approved lore or implemented content. This document adds playable mission design to the [Level 1–80 Progression Prototype](Level_1_80_Progression_Prototype.md) and [Human quest migration register](Human_1_80_Quest_Migration_Register.md). Existing 92 principal quests and 24 optional quests remain in their source files, untouched.

## Design conventions

- IDs `N01`–`N73` are **provisional new quest-design IDs**, not production database IDs. Each entry has a contact and concrete completion objective; dialogue, encounters, exact coordinates, item rewards, XP and tuning require a later pass.
- The existing original quest IDs listed at each chapter are **reuse inputs**, not a claim that their old level tags or dependencies remain valid. Rebuild prerequisites in the new chapter order.
- Main-story chapters require minimum level and prior story chapter, but ordinary levelling does not require main-story completion. Regional quests, exploration, combat, gathering and optional dungeons must provide alternative XP.
- Preserve WoW Classic-style target, facing and autoattack fundamentals; solo critical story, fixed-route ships/flight paths and overland travel; no mandatory WvW, raid or class-specific casting.
- Existing story outcomes take precedence where locked. The 1–80 prototype's precise level placements and successful Major Anchor inversion are still Needs Review. In particular, the founder deaths and Oth'varen's eventual death are dated locks, while encounter implementation is not.
- The prototype budgets 163 main-story **steps**, not necessarily 163 standalone quests. Existing quests plus these new entries exceed that as authored titles; consolidation/splitting is expected in production. Do not mistake counts for tuned scope.

## Mandatory reconciliation before production

1. The original `H2-14` relies on Brynja's detailed Kar Durn inspection, which the new chronology moves later. Supply an early technical note or shift that dependency; do not present a character as already recruited before their introduction.
2. Original `H3-16` destroys/isolate-shuts a Regional Anchor, while new Act II needs a tested inversion and capture of a different Major Anchor. Keep installations, scale and consequences distinct.
3. Original `H4-15` featured a projection and records, not Su'vareth's death. `N40`–`N43` must be written as a separate verified final operation, not a retroactive change to the old dungeon.
4. Original `H5-16` permitted safe severance; the new 30–35 Major inversion outcome is not yet creator-ratified. Keep a clear review gate before locking that encounter.
5. Do not treat the proposed Veil/Shroud failure mechanism or Nhal manifestation limits as established technical canon without the planned lore pass.
6. Ensure the 35–40 Shroud expedition retains the separate regional chains from the existing progression prototype; no story-locked Shroud zone is necessary to reach 40 by XP.
7. Preserve a distinct level-80 finale after reaching cap. `N69`–`N73` represent finale/aftermath beats, not XP required to unlock level 80.
8. Each new quest still needs: start/turn-in positions, prerequisites, objective type, enemy archetypes and AI, retry/failure rules, travel distance, estimated duration, XP/item rewards, and any local-world persistence.
