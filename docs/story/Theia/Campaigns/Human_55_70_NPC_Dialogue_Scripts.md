## Continuity and performance rules

Khor'vun operates a distributed Anchor network that amplifies the original tear. The Anchors are **not** the Binding or prison. Vaer'uun knowingly serves Nhal under corruption and manipulates the player using genuine omissions from Oth'varen's account. Nhal's name is authenticated at Q143. Keeper deceived the Seven, including Guardian; Guardian discovered the truth after the Binding. Q156 is a **combat victory but strategic failure**: the player kills Khor'vun after the irreversible threshold; Veilbreaker destroys the Veil, the Binding immediately dissolves because it has nothing to contain, the Seven are freed, and Shroud dissipation starts immediately. Nhal's manifestation develops slowly.

## Mandatory cinematic dialogue sequencing

**Q140 — Invitation:** Vaer'uun must offer a verifiable truth and a location. His manipulation works by asking why Oth'varen concealed the Binding, not by demanding blind loyalty. The player may react with suspicion, curiosity or anger; the invitation is investigated regardless.

**Q142 — Oth'varen confronted:** He admits withholding knowledge without claiming Vaer'uun's conclusions are correct. The player has a credible reason to doubt him while still valuing his help.

**Q145 — First Voice:** Vaer'uun knows he serves Nhal; corruption has twisted his judgment, not made him an unaware pawn. He dies permanently.

**Q149–Q151 — Keeper's deception:** independent sources establish what Keeper knew, what the other gods understood, and Guardian's later discovery. Avoid claiming the Anchors maintain the Binding.

**Q155–Q157 — Veilbreaker:** the threshold is already irreversible on arrival. Khor'vun remains a genuine boss victory. After his death, Veil destruction and Binding dissolution are immediate and causal, while the Shroud's physical disappearance progresses over time. Show the Seven freed without implying their essence is destroyed. Nhal's entry is gradual.
