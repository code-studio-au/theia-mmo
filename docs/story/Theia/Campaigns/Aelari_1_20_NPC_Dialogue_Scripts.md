# Theia — Aelari Levels 1–20 NPC Dialogue Scripts, Pass 1

**Campaign status:** The First Hunt is approved for further work. This authoring draft contains 18 main quests and 16 side quests. Dialogue, precise geography, rewards and XP remain subject to review. IDs are editorial references.

**Dialogue design:** Text-first, short lines. No player name, voice or gender assumed. Choices change tone only. Main quests unlock in numbered order. Side quests are independent except for the two Sylvaren pairs, whose second quests follow their first, and the game hall quest, which opens after the family reaches Ridgewatch. Both city pairs open on arrival and remain available after the finale.

## Boughstead

[Zone note](../Atlas/Places/Boughstead.md)

### AE1-01 — Before the First Hunt

**Scene and objectives:** Village trailhead to Maerin’s orchard. Lift the broken fence rail, free the loaded cart, examine the damaged rows and question a farmhand.

**Talan, offer:** “Bring your bow. Maerin needs a hand before we head into the woods.”

**Maerin, scene:** “Ripe enough to fall, and they ran right over it.”

**Talan, completion:** “Walk both ends of the trail. Let’s see where they started running.”

### AE1-02 — Patrol to the Woodland Edge

**Scene and objectives:** Brace the damaged gate on the lower path, follow the boar prints to the trees and sketch the larger tracks where the herd turned.

**Talan, offer:** “Take the lower path. Check the gate while you’re there, then follow the prints back.”

**Talan, scene:** “See where the stride shortens? They all turned together.”

**Talan, completion:** “Bear. Show Neris where those tracks come up against the ward.”

### AE1-03 — The Storehouse and the Ledger

**Scene and objectives:** Reset the shifted marker, trace small prints to the storehouse, clear the yard and open the rear door. Nivelle takes the first cub into care. Copy Maerin’s ledger entry about the ward extension and last season’s boar drive.

**Neris, offer:** “Hold the stone upright. Look at the scrape along the base while I clear its seat.”

**Farmhand, scene:** “We closed the gap at dawn. Thought the noise inside was the cat.”

**Nivelle, scene:** “Leave the bowl where it can see it.”

**Talan, completion:** “One cub safe. Those other prints go uphill. First, there’s trouble at the lower cottages.”

### AE1-04 — The Lower Cottages

**Scene and objectives:** Find Faerun at the damaged cottages. Clear a branch from the cart lane and help the family move their belongings to the road.

**Talan, offer:** “Go down with Faerun. Find out whether the other homes are still occupied.”

**Faerun, scene:** “She came through twice. The second time, I heard something calling up on the ridge.”

**Talan, completion:** “I’ll mark that for Rhael. Check the east path before the rest of the households move.”

### AE1-05 — The East Footpath

**Scene and objectives:** Clear the blocked section, mark the detour at both ends and walk it with a resident carrying supplies. Move the marker until it can be seen before the fork.

**Neris, offer:** “The lower lane is shut. People are cutting through a stock pen to get home.”

**Neris, scene:** “Stand where they have to choose. Which way would you go from here?”

**Neris, completion:** “They can see it now. Go back for Faerun’s family.”

### AE1-06 — The Road to Ridgewatch

**Scene and objectives:** Escort the family through the glades to the game hall. Give the sketch to Rhael and compare two witness accounts.

**Talan, offer:** “Take the sketch to Rhael. Walk in with the family. They’ve had enough people talking over them from horseback.”

**Witness, scene:** “I heard a cub above the path.”

**Second witness, scene:** “The mother turned back toward the lower woods.”

**Rhael, completion:** “There’s a shelf above the old den. Get them by the hearth, then we’ll look.”

### AE1-B01 — The Missing Press Frame

**Scene and objectives:** Find the borrowed frame at the cooper’s yard, fetch a sound brace and help fit it before returning the frame to the press.

**Maerin, offer:** “If the cooper still needs it, ask what’s holding him up. The fruit’s ready.”

**Cooper, scene:** “You can have it as soon as the brace holds. Pick a straight length from that rack.”

**Maerin, completion:** “Set it by the press. We can start before the next cart comes in.”

### AE1-B02 — The Orchard Ledger

**Scene and objectives:** Count two growers’ crates, inspect the dry and sheltered bays, then propose a split based on which harvest is already damp.

**Maerin, offer:** “They both have the same bay written down. Come tell me what each one actually needs.”

**Grower, scene:** “Mine can wait a day. Hers was picked in the rain.”

**Maerin, completion:** “I’ll put both names by the new spaces. They can come straight in with their carts.”

### AE1-B03 — A Path for the Healer

**Scene and objectives:** Clear the approach off the west glade, deliver the medicine basket and bring back the household’s empty jars.

**Nivelle, offer:** “Carry it level. There’s a jar in there I’ve already replaced twice.”

**Resident, scene:** “Leave the gate open. I’ll bring the empty jars out myself.”

**Nivelle, completion:** “Good. I can take the short path on my next round.”

### AE1-B04 — The Loose Flock

**Scene and objectives:** Scout the fork, deal with the predator near the fold and drive each scattered group back through the open gate.

**Shepherd, offer:** “They’ve split between the tracks. Check the lower fold before you push them that way.”

**Shepherd, scene:** “Leave that gate wide. They’ll bunch up if you stand in it.”

**Shepherd, completion:** “That’s the last of them. Hold the gate while I count.”

### AE1-B05 — Along the Old Ward Line

**Scene and objectives:** Match three weathered markers to a sketch and trace the footpath the old layout kept open.

**Neris, offer:** “The sketch is older than the orchard. Find the stones before you try to read the line.”

**Neris, scene:** “Look where they left room to walk. That’ll tell you who used it.”

**Neris, completion:** “Bring me a copy with that path drawn in. The old one stops at the orchard edge.”

## Ridgewatch

[Zone note](../Atlas/Places/Ridgewatch.md)

### AE1-07 — The Ledge Above the Den

**Scene and objectives:** Scout from the upper trail, mark the return path, defeat the wolves blocking the approach and recover the second cub. Bring it to Thaelis.

**Rhael, offer:** “Stop where the brush thins. If you see the cub, mark your way out before you go down.”

**Rhael, scene:** “There, on the shelf. Wait until the way below is clear.”

**Thaelis, completion:** “Set it here. Saela’s awake on the next cot. She’s been asking who followed the bear.”

### AE1-08 — The Watchkeeper’s Account

**Scene and objectives:** Speak to Saela at her cot. Hear Talan’s response and record where she stood and where the bear was trying to go. Bring the marked route to Rhael.

**Thaelis, offer:** “Saela heard you found a cub. She wants to tell you where the mother was heading.”

**Saela, scene:** “She passed two people. I stepped across the lower track and she struck.”

**Talan, scene:** “And I’m supposed to give her another chance?”

**Saela, completion:** “Find where she was going before you send someone else to stand in her way.”

### AE1-09 — The Watchkeeper’s Patrol

**Scene and objectives:** Walk Saela’s route past two occupied cottages. Clear a damaged rail from the footpath and follow the overlapping tracks to a wooded hollow.

**Rhael, offer:** “Show me exactly where Saela stood. We’ll walk her route from the top.”

**Rhael, scene:** “She could have gone into either yard. She kept taking this turn.”

**Rhael, completion:** “There’s canvas down there. Let’s see what’s left of the camp.”

### AE1-10 — The Camp She Came Back To

**Scene and objectives:** Examine the wreckage, cage latch with cub fur and wagon ruts crossing the bear prints. Record where the wagon joined the hauling road.

**Rhael, offer:** “Look at the ground before you move anything. What was here when she came through?”

**Rhael, scene:** “Two cubs in care. This one left in a cage.”

**Rhael, completion:** “A loaded wagon can only take a few of those forks. Start with the hauling road.”

### AE1-11 — The Road the Wagon Took

**Scene and objectives:** Check the hauling road junctions and question Veyrien. Take the wagon description and gate information to Edrin at Sylvaren’s outer post.

**Rhael, offer:** “Follow the easier grade. Ask Veyrien whether a wagon passed after the camp was wrecked.”

**Veyrien, scene:** “He asked which gate stays open after dusk. I told him. Thought he was hauling tools.”

**Edrin, completion:** “I’ll alert the next post. Ask the timber crews which yard took the wagon.”

### AE1-R01 — A Bed at the Game Hall

**Scene and objectives:** After arriving in Ridgewatch, find a place for the family beyond tonight, collect their things from the storeroom and help them move.

**Faerun, offer:** “My youngest finally slept. If there’s a way to stay here another night, I’ll take it.”

**Hall keeper, scene:** “Two beds by the east wall. Bring their blankets over before supper.”

**Faerun, completion:** “Tell the keeper we can help with breakfast. We’ll be up anyway.”

### AE1-R02 — The Unmarked Spur

**Scene and objectives:** Locate the fallen signs and the washed-out section, mark the passable approach and guide a packer through the fork.

**Veyrien, offer:** “Walk the spur before you trust either sign. I haven’t seen it since the rain.”

**Veyrien, scene:** “If a sign sends you toward loose shale, take it down before someone trusts it.”

**Packer, completion:** “I can see both markers from here. I’ll tell the next driver which way to turn.”

### AE1-R03 — What the Forest Provides

**Scene and objectives:** Accompany a novice, identify similar plants by habitat, gather from a mature patch and leave its young growth in place.

**Herbalist, offer:** “Take the novice with you. I want them to remember where the plants grow.”

**Herbalist, scene:** “Tell me where you found it before you tell me what it is.”

**Novice, completion:** “The young shoots are still there. I’ll show you the patch next time.”

### AE1-R04 — Under the Slate Shelf

**Scene and objectives:** Scout an approach around a fallen slate face, defeat predators below the shelf and lead both stranded workers out.

**Quarry foreworker, offer:** “Two of my crew are still up there. See if the eastern track reaches them.”

**Worker, scene:** “There’s another voice up here. Don’t go back with just me.”

**Foreworker, completion:** “Both accounted for. Stay for a drink while I close the track.”

### AE1-R05 — The Ridgewatch Night Watch

**Scene and objectives:** On a dusk patrol, check the pens, relight the post lamp and secure the gate struck open by livestock.

**Watchkeeper, offer:** “Take the upland circuit. If the post is dark, light it before you go past.”

**Watchkeeper, scene:** “The lamp tells people there’s someone here. Light it first.”

**Watchkeeper, completion:** “Gate’s holding, lamp’s lit. I’ll take the next round.”

### AE1-R06 — The Healer’s Round

**Scene and objectives:** Visit three patients, deliver their supplies, arrange a return visit for one worker and report the household unable to reach the infirmary.

**Thaelis, offer:** “Three stops. Find out which of them can get here under their own strength.”

**Thaelis, scene:** “Ask who’s changing the dressing. If they say “I am,” ask them to show you.”

**Thaelis, completion:** “I’ll go to the last house myself. Leave their supplies where I can pick them up.”

## Sylvaren

[Zone note](../Atlas/Places/Sylvaren.md)

### AE1-12 — The Covered Load

**Scene and objectives:** Compare watch reports with the timber crews’ accounts. Sireth identifies an enclosed yard. Check both gates from the public lane and report back.

**Edrin, offer:** “Find the crew that was working when the wagon came through.”

**Sireth, scene:** “It scraped the boards and cried out. He said it was a bad wheel. I’ve heard bad wheels.”

**Edrin, completion:** “My watch will cover both exits. You go for the cub.”

### AE1-13 — Before They Move It

**Scene and objectives:** Enter as the watch secures the gates. Stop the transfer, free the third cub and take it to Lethiel. Edrin takes the trappers into custody.

**Edrin, offer:** “They’re shifting the load to a second wagon. We’ll close the gates when you’re in position.”

**Trapper, scene:** “We took one. The other two bolted. Then she tore through the camp.”

**Lethiel, completion:** “Water and quiet first. It’ll be fit to travel after that. Where are you taking it?”

### AE1-14 — The Old Crossing

**Scene and objectives:** Walk the likely return route through the outer orchards. Compare the blocked crossing with Maerin’s ledger and show her where the tracks turn toward the houses.

**Saela, offer:** “Walk with me as far as the old crossing. I want to see where she’d go coming back this way.”

**Maerin, scene:** “I signed off on it. We’d just planted the outer rows.”

**Maerin, completion:** “I knew exactly where my ward ended. I should have walked farther.”

### AE1-15 — The Houses Below the Crossing

**Scene and objectives:** Walk the proposed route with Avariel. Identify the branch leading to household doors, mark an uphill alternative and take the report to Neris.

**Saela, offer:** “Ask Avariel to show you the path from the houses. We’ve only walked it from the orchard.”

**Avariel, scene:** “See that worn step? Children come home that way. Move the opening uphill and I’ll help clear it.”

**Neris, completion:** “That keeps the homes inside the line. Maerin will have to give up the outer rows.”

### AE1-16 — Room at the Boundary

**Scene and objectives:** Survey the revised line with Neris, move the ward markers and confirm the branch to the houses stays protected. Edrin arranges transport from each cub’s shelter.

**Maerin, offer:** “Set it inside the rows. I’ll tell the growers myself.”

**Neris, scene:** “Look through to the woods. Then check the turn toward the houses before we set the last stone.”

**Edrin, completion:** “All three healers have cleared their cubs to travel. My watch will bring them when you’re ready.”

### AE1-17 — Three Cubs at the Crossing

**Scene and objectives:** In a solo story encounter, clear the trail, help set the final marker and hold position with Talan as the mother collects her cubs.

**Neris, offer:** “Check the trail before the handlers bring them in. I’ll seat the stone once the path is clear.”

**Talan, scene:** “Bows down. Give her the whole path.”

**Talan, completion:** “I was ready to send hunters after her again. Saela will want to hear how this ended. Come tell her yourself.”

### AE1-18 — The First Hunt Meal

**Scene and objectives:** Return to the hall in Sylvaren. Speak with Faerun and Maerin, tell the apprentices what you noticed in the orchard, and take your place at Talan’s table.

**Talan, offer:** “They’ve saved you a seat. Saela wants the apprentices to hear how it started.”

**Saela, scene:** “You’ve heard about the bear. Ask where the hunt really started.”

**Talan, completion:** “Next time an apprentice walks those rows, you take them. Sit here tonight.”

### AE1-S01 — No Easy Climb

**Scene and objectives:** Deliver dressings to a walkway worker via the lower path around the closed stairs. Ask the crew how long the clinic detour takes.

**Lethiel, offer:** “Take these to the work shelter. The usual stair is closed.”

**Worker, scene:** “I can manage the stairs or carry my tools. Today I can’t do both.”

**Lethiel, completion:** “If one worker is putting off a visit, others are too. Find Ilyen below the walks.”

### AE1-S02 — A Table Below

**Scene and objectives:** Choose a sheltered spot with Ilyen, bring down supplies and help Lethiel set up her first treatment round.

**Lethiel, offer:** “Ilyen knows where the crews can stop safely. Ask her for somewhere dry and clear of carts.”

**Ilyen, scene:** “Use that alcove. The crew eats there when it rains. They’ll know where to find you.”

**Lethiel, completion:** “Leave the spare dressings here. I’ll be back for the next shift.”

### AE1-S03 — Carts in the Footlane

**Scene and objectives:** Walk the delivery route with Rennel and a carter. Find the temporary works sign still directing carts into the pedestrian lane.

**Edrin, offer:** “Go with Rennel. Walk it from the driver’s end before you move a sign.”

**Carter, scene:** “The sign says this way. The watch says the other. Which one do I trust with a loaded cart behind me?”

**Rennel, completion:** “That’s a works sign. Find Ilyen before we send a cart down the old road.”

### AE1-S04 — The Proper Road

**Scene and objectives:** Confirm the carriageway is ready with Ilyen, clear the approved barriers and reset both signs. Guide the first cart to the market gate and report to Edrin.

**Rennel, offer:** “Walk the whole delivery road with me. We reopen it once Ilyen clears the barriers.”

**Ilyen, scene:** “I can spare two hands to move the barriers. If you want a third, bring gloves.”

**Edrin, completion:** “The market cart is through. Rennel can leave that junction to the regular watch.”

### AE1-S05 — The Water Gate

**Scene and objectives:** Trace the existing channels to a stuck gate. Free it and rebalance the flow, then inspect the dry and waterlogged beds.

**Nursery cultivator, offer:** “One tray’s dry and the other’s standing in water. Walk the channels before you touch the gate.”

**Cultivator, scene:** “Open it a finger’s width. I want a drink for those roots, not a bath.”

**Cultivator, completion:** “That’s enough. Come back when the seedlings are ready to move.”

## Encounter and state handoffs

Record each cub at its own shelter upon completion of AE1-03, AE1-07 and AE1-13. The crossing unlocks after all three rescues and the boundary survey. Stage the cubs when the solo reunion starts. A failed or interrupted attempt can replay the encounter from entry without resetting earlier rescues. On completion, the bear leaves with all three and the meal becomes available. City-side work, access to services and the delivery road chain remain available independently of the reunion. Ilyen can greet the player according to the first chain in which they met.
