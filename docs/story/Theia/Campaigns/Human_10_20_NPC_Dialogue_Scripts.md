## Character and knowledge direction

Cassian must initially appear helpful and competent, not a theatrical villain. Aldren is grieving Edric and wants lawful evidence. Mara prizes corroboration over intuition. Garrick opposes peace without being guilty of Edric's death. No one in this chapter knows the full Unmade conspiracy, Nhal, the Binding or the Kel'vari. The player has no mandatory gendered voice or dialogue. Optional regional stories remain ordinary life.
