# Human 1–80 — Quest Migration Register

**Needs Review — 2 November 2026.** Editorial migration proposal, not approved canon, implemented gameplay or tested XP. Based on the [original 92-quest register](../../THEIA_HUMAN_QUEST_REGISTER.md) and [level 1–80 prototype](Level_1_80_Progression_Prototype.md). The original files remain untouched.

**Rules:** Each of the 92 original main quests has exactly one primary destination; a destination is a traceability mapping, not a new production quest ID. Original 24 optional local quests are mapped separately. Minimum-level chapters unlock at 1, 5, 10 and every five levels thereafter, with story prerequisites, while normal levelling remains independent of the main plot. Ordinary travel uses ships, fixed flight paths and overland routes. Solo access to essential story is preserved.

## Work not covered by the original quests

The new prototype budgets **163 main-story steps**, **456 selected regional/local steps**, and an **816-slot regional/local authoring envelope** across levels 1–80, plus provisional finale/aftermath units. The 92 original quests are not equivalent to 92 of those steps: some split, some merge, and many new steps are required. The 24 old local quests are only a starting inventory. XP and playtime figures are hypotheses, not tuned production values.

- **1–20:** Flesh out independent Millhaven, Stoneford and Valecrown regional routes; move H1-17 into the level-15 chapter without losing evidence-gathering.
- **20–30:** Repair the Brynja continuity problem caused by moving Kar Durn after Silent Signal; develop real capital-region activities and conventional overseas access.
- **30–35:** Author Oma'vira's real facility, captive rescue and death, and a Major Anchor inversion with bounded safeguards; this replaces the old Act V finale's outcome.
- **35–45:** Expand the existing Shroud 35–40 regional prototype and Kel'vari archives, ensuring alternative XP outside story-locked regions.
- **45–50:** Author Su'vareth's independently corroborated headquarters, final deception, permanent death and surviving contingency orders.
- **50–60:** New Veilbreaker defended approaches, Khor'vun death, evacuation, activation and distinct technical causes for Veil and Shroud collapse.
- **60–65:** New Nhal manifestations, freed-god encounters, Vaer'uun's death and post-catastrophe regional recovery.
- **65–75:** New Keeper disclosure, informed Seven alignment, distributed inversion maintenance, teams and transport logistics.
- **75–80 + finale:** New Thes'var confrontation/death, Oth'varen's fatal stabilisation, final Nhal shattering and post-finale homecoming.

## Approval and continuity flags

1. **The 1–80 structure remains Needs Review.** Do not treat its proposed Major Anchor inversion, level timing or endgame as newly creator-locked merely because this crosswalk is committed.
2. The dated Conclave outcomes govern: Oma'vira, Su'vareth, Khor'vun, Vaer'uun and Thes'var die in that order; Oth'varen dies after Thes'var's interference and before Nhal's defeat. The older draft's all-founders-survive ending is superseded.
3. Preserve independent evidence, lawful handling of Cassian, Covenant/Dominion separation, Kel'vari sovereignty and non-conspiracy local problems.
4. Every main chapter needs solo-accessible critical evidence, safe retries and optional PvP/raid variants only; no class or profession gate.
5. Verify regional XP availability, travel time, prerequisites, NPC continuity and chapter playtime in a separate design pass before assigning final new IDs.

**Validation:** 92 distinct original principal IDs assigned exactly once; 24 optional IDs represented; 16 levelling chapters; original source documents unmodified.
