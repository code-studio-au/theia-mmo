**Status: Needs Review; original dialogue draft, not localized or engine-integrated.** Canonical story: [Human 1–80 Canonical Campaign](Human_1_80_Canonical_Campaign.md). Structured stages: [Human 45–55 Quest Data](Human_1_80_Current_Quest_Authoring_Manifest.json).

## Narrative guardrails

Su'vareth orchestrated the Shattered Delegation, planting conflicting evidence to inflame both factions. The Dominion High Ruler's brother was a Great Clan chief, opposed peace and was reluctantly persuaded by the High Ruler to attend as an official delegate; he was killed in the attack. The player has **no friendly Dominion interactions**: Q108 is hostile archive infiltration. Su'vareth dies permanently at Q115. Oth'varen learns of the modern Veilbreaker operation through Su'vareth's recovered orders at Q118; he did not know the plan in advance. Anchors amplify the original tear and are not the Seven's Binding. The full Keeper/Binding truth remains hidden until later.

- **Q115:** Su'vareth's argument is manipulation, not moral exoneration. Author separate boss barks and phase triggers before implementation.
- **Q118:** Oth'varen explicitly distinguishes ancient technical expertise from foreknowledge of the modern operation.
- **Q125:** Tarek's distinction between Anchors and Binding must remain unambiguous.
- **Q128:** chapter ends with preparations, not Veilbreaker's success. Khor'vun's decisive event is near level 70.
