## Narrative direction

- Q158–Q162: Nhal's manifestation is gradual. Divine essence is indestructible; inversion fractures coherent identity and memory, not the substance. Oth'varen must not imply certainty about the future of fragments.
- Q163: Thes'var knowingly serves Nhal under corruption; his argument is rationalized wrongdoing, not a secret rescue plan.
- Q164–Q165: bounded tests support coordinated inversion but do not prove its final result.
- Q170: Oth'varen does **not** know he will die. Training independent teams reflects his long-standing concern about hoarded knowledge.
- Q171: Covenant and Kel'vari permissions are distinct; no one grants sovereignty to the other.
- Q174: finale only begins at level 80.
