**Status: Needs Review; original authored first-pass dialogue, not voice-ready or engine-integrated.** Narrative authority: [Human 1–80 Canonical Campaign](Human_1_80_Canonical_Campaign.md). Quest stages: [Human 35–45 Quest Data](Human_1_80_Current_Quest_Authoring_Manifest.json).

## Reveal and character boundaries

Sera has knowingly concealed a restricted Guardian gate, but she is not a secret enemy. The gate is an exceptional low-capacity Passage connection and requires Kel'vari consent. The Kel'vari are almost erased from outside history and are first encountered alive in mandatory Q086; the player must not know their name from public dialogue beforehand. Lantern Redoubt is a longstanding joint outpost, not a new Covenant fortress. Oth'varen is revealed as the resistant sixth scholar at Q097 by independent verification; he is knowledgeable, not omniscient. No Nhal or Binding truth is prematurely disclosed.

## Scene-specific direction

**Q081:** Sera's confession must be uncomfortable. She explains her duty but does not demand absolution. The player may respond with anger, pragmatism or cautious understanding; all lead to the same permission process.

**Q084–Q086:** The custodians, not Sera, authorize the crossing. Reveal the Kel'vari visually and through Aru'ven's introduction at Lantern Redoubt. No sea route, ordinary flight, or walkable land connection can substitute for the gate.

**Q094–Q097:** The player discovers the verifier's mark before Oth'varen admits his identity. The revelation should feel earned, not an exposition dump.

**Q102–Q103:** The Shattered Delegation investigation begins from contradictions, not a prior assumption that Dominion authorities will cooperate. Later Dominion records are obtained through hostile infiltration.
