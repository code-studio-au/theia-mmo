# THEIA — Human 1–80 Canonical Campaign and Quest Register

**Status: LOCKED narrative and quest-order baseline — 2 November 2026.** Creator-approved decisions from the current story review take precedence over superseded 1–60 and provisional 1–80 drafts. Quest titles, chapter placement, objectives and locations below are the canonical authoring baseline; detailed dialogue, combat implementation, regional XP counts and encounter balance remain to be produced. This document is the **single source of truth** for the Human launch campaign on this branch.

## Non-negotiable story rules

- The Anchor network is **not the prison**. Unmade exploit Anchors to amplify the original tear in the Veil. The Seven's Binding contains the Veil; the Shroud is its visible effect.
- Khor'vun's Veilbreaker **destroys the Veil at approximately level 70**. The Binding **immediately dissolves** because there is no Veil left to contain. The Seven are freed immediately, survive with identities intact, and the Shroud begins dissipating immediately. Nhal's manifestation in the Physical Realm is gradual.
- The Unmade knowingly serve Nhal under long-standing corruption. After release, they work to stabilize Nhal's coherent manifestation; Thes'var is not secretly repairing the world.
- Divine essence cannot be annihilated. Coordinated inversion can shatter Nhal's coherent identity, will and memory, leaving divine essence in fragments. Their future is reserved for an expansion; no launch explanation of their destiny.
- At level 80, Thes'var dies in combat and his sabotage misaligns convergence. **Only then** Oth'varen realizes the correction will kill him. He tells Sera and the player, physically dies after seeing alignment hold, and the Seven and mortal teams finish Nhal's shattering. The Seven survive and Covenant–Dominion war remains unresolved.
- The Guardians have a secret, low-capacity ancient Passage gate maintained with Kel'vari consent. Oma'vira's gate was destroyed. The Shroud cannot be reached by normal shipping, walking or flight. Lantern Redoubt is a longstanding restricted Guardian–Kel'vari outpost. Kel'vari were nearly erased from outside history; their existence is a major player revelation at 35–40.
- Human characters never receive friendly questing access to Dominion rulers, capitals or military authorities. Hostile infiltration of Dominion military records is allowed. The Great Clan chief killed at the Shattered Delegation was the Dominion High Ruler's brother, reluctantly attending as an official delegate despite opposing peace.
- Main-story critical content remains solo-accessible; story chapters have minimum levels and prior-story prerequisites. Ordinary levelling can use regional content without main-story completion. Travel uses ships, fixed-route flight paths, onward foot/mount travel, and rare restricted Passage gates.
- Main-story choices can affect reactions, follow-up scenes and optional exhibits without fragmenting the shared canonical outcome. Keep original evidence-custody and safe-retry rules.
