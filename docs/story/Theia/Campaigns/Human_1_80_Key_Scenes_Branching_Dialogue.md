## SCENE H35-081 — Sera admits the Guardian gate exists

**Prerequisites:** Q079 complete; Oma'vira's destroyed gate record in evidence journal. **Location:** Guardian sanctuary, private records room. **Participants:** Sera, player. **Objective:** complete Q081; open Q082 and permission path.

**Blocking:** Sera stands beside an open ledger. No music swell until she confirms the hidden gate. The player remains free to move until dialogue begins; camera cuts only when the player chooses to speak.

**Sera:** “You are right. We have a Passage gate. It survived the Sundering. We kept it working, quietly, with the permission of those beyond it.”

**Player A — angry:** “You sent me into danger while hiding this?”

**Sera A:** “Yes. I decided what you could know. I told myself it was protection. I won't ask you to call that fair.”

**Player B — practical:** “Who controls access?”

**Sera B:** “Not the Guardians alone. We maintain the mechanism. Its custodians decide who may cross.”

**Player C — cautious:** “Why keep it secret?”

**Sera C:** “Because those beyond it asked for secrecy, and the world has a history of treating discovery as a claim.”

**Shared continuation, Sera:** “You may read the maintenance record. After that, if you still wish to cross, we must ask permission.”

**Flags:** `SERA_GATE_RESPONSE=ANGRY|PRACTICAL|CAUTIOUS`; Q081 completes on shared continuation. **Do not reveal:** Kel'vari name, their surviving society, Oth'varen identity.

## SCENE H35-084/086 — Consent and first living Kel'vari encounter

**Q084, Guardian sanctuary:** The speaker is identified only as **Custodian** in player-visible text. No racial title or portrait spoiler.

**Custodian:** “You ask to enter a land that has paid dearly for being known. The gate is not yours to open merely because you found it.”

**Player A:** “I accept your conditions.” → **Custodian:** “Then enter as a guest, with a limited party and a defined purpose.”

**Player B:** “What if we need to bring soldiers?” → **Custodian:** “Then the answer is no. This is not a military road.”

**Player C:** “How do I know the gate is safe?” → **Custodian:** “You do not. We will show you what we have measured and what remains uncertain.”

**Q085:** Tarek verifies capacity, return sequence and consent. The player crosses with a small approved group.

**Q086, Lantern Redoubt:** First camera reveal of living Kel'vari custodians performing everyday work, not standing in a ceremonial line waiting for the player. Aru'ven steps forward.

**Aru'ven:** “You have crossed into our homeland. We are Kel'vari. The outside world remembers our absence more clearly than our lives.”

**Player A:** “I thought your people were gone.” → **Aru'ven:** “Many were lost. Those who remained built lives, not merely hiding places.”

**Player B:** “Why allow us here?” → **Aru'ven:** “Because the danger has reached us too. Cooperation is not surrender.”

**Player C:** “What is this place?” → **Aru'ven:** “Lantern Redoubt. A small place of contact, kept by our custodians and the Guardians. Not a fortress for your armies.”

**Shared:** The player is registered as a visitor; access limits persist. No sovereignty transfer.

## SCENE H40-097 — The Grey Surveyor is Oth'varen

**Prerequisites:** Q094–Q096 verifier evidence collected. **Location:** Oth'irun private research chamber. **Participants:** Grey Surveyor, Sel'ora, player.

**Player:** “The verifier's mark on the original project is yours.”

**Grey Surveyor:** “I hoped that would remain a question longer.”

**Player A — accusation:** “You watched us search for you.” → **Surveyor:** “I did. I was afraid of what my answer would make you ask next.”

**Player B — curiosity:** “How are you still alive?” → **Surveyor:** “Not unchanged. Survival is not the same as escaping what happened.”

**Player C — trust:** “Why not tell us?” → **Surveyor:** “Because I wanted my work judged before my history.”

**Shared reveal:** “My name is Oth'varen. I was the sixth scholar. The others accepted what I refused.”

**Sel'ora:** “His mark is independently verified. His account still requires scrutiny.”

**Flags:** `OTHVAREN_REVEAL_TONE=ACCUSATION|CURIOSITY|TRUST`. Player-facing speaker label changes from **Grey Surveyor** to **Oth'varen** only after the shared reveal.

## SCENE H60-140 — Vaer'uun's invitation

**Prerequisites:** First Voice research, missing records. **Location:** authorized ancient communication chamber.

**Vaer'uun:** “You have searched the past beside the scholar who survived us. Has he shown you why the gods were bound? Or only why he should be believed?”

**Player A — distrust:** “You serve the thing that corrupted you.” → **Vaer'uun:** “And yet I have given you a place where the records can be checked. Ask why he has not.”

**Player B — skeptical:** “A real record can still be a trap.” → **Vaer'uun:** “Of course. Bring instruments. Bring witnesses. I have no need to ask you to believe me.”

**Player C — curious:** “What will I find?” → **Vaer'uun:** “A truth that will make silence difficult to defend.”

**Shared:** Vaer'uun transmits a verifiable destination and ends the connection. Sera orders independent reconnaissance. The player does not join Vaer'uun and does not learn Nhal's name here.

## SCENE H60-142 — Oth'varen answers for withholding evidence

**Oth'varen:** “The records are genuine. I withheld them because I feared what someone might do with half the history.”

**Player A:** “You made that choice for us.” → **Oth'varen:** “Yes. That is what I cannot defend.”

**Player B:** “Did Vaer'uun tell the truth?” → **Oth'varen:** “About these records, yes. About what they justify, no.”

**Player C:** “Can we still work together?” → **Oth'varen:** “If you insist on evidence rather than my authority, perhaps we can.”

**Shared:** Oth'varen grants restricted archive access, not a full Binding revelation before Q149–Q151.

## SCENE H65-151 — The Seven Bound

**Prerequisites:** Keeper's omitted testimony and Guardian's post-Binding discovery independently authenticated.

**Oth'varen:** “Keeper deceived the others. Guardian did not know what was being asked of them until the Binding had already been made.”

**Player A:** “Then the gods were prisoners?” → **Oth'varen:** “They were bound to a purpose they had not freely understood.”

**Player B:** “What does that make the Shroud?” → **Oth'varen:** “The visible face of the Binding. The Veil was what it contained.”

**Player C:** “And the Anchors?” → **Oth'varen:** “Separate machinery. They were manipulated to widen the original tear. They never held the Seven.”

**Shared:** “If the Veil is destroyed, the Binding has nothing left to contain. It cannot continue serving its purpose.”

## SCENE H65-156 — Veilbreaker: victory too late

**Pre-boss:** Khor'vun stands at a control assembly already past the irreversible threshold. Player can inspect readings before engaging.

**Khor'vun:** “You are not early enough to change what has begun.”

**Player:** “Then I'll stop you.”

**Khor'vun:** “You can stop me.”

**Encounter:** Khor'vun is defeated in a winnable solo fight. No forced defeat. Player attempts cutoff. It fails because activation is already complete.

**Sera:** “The Veil is gone. The Binding—it's dissolving. The Seven are free.”

**Tarek:** “The Shroud boundary is withdrawing. It started the moment the Veil failed.”

**Oth'varen:** “Nhal is no longer held beyond it. But manifestation will take time. We must use that time.”

**World stage:** Shroud dissipation starts immediately. The Seven survive. Nhal's physical presence grows gradually.

## SCENE H80-176/181 — The Architect's defeat and the Sixth Scholar's choice

**Q176, Thes'var confrontation**

**Thes'var:** “You call this a prison broken. I call it a design finally allowed to reach its purpose.”

**Player:** “You know what Nhal will do.”

**Thes'var:** “I know whom I serve. I know what I have chosen.”

**Combat:** Thes'var dies. His final sabotage becomes apparent afterward, not before.

**Q177, Sera:** “The controls have shifted. The safe correction fails every time we try it.”

**Q178, Oth'varen:** “There is one correction left. It must be made inside the convergence.”

**Sera:** “The exposure will kill you.”

**Oth'varen:** “I know that now. I did not know it when we came.”

**Q179, farewell choices**

**Player A — protest:** “There has to be another way.” → **Oth'varen:** “We searched for it. If you find one, use it. We have no time left to search.”

**Player B — grief:** “I don't want you to die.” → **Oth'varen:** “Neither do I. That is why this is a choice, not a destiny.”

**Player C — resolve:** “Tell me what you need.” → **Oth'varen:** “Hold the stations. Protect Sera and the others. Let me reach the junction.”

**Sera:** “I won't call this fair.”

**Oth'varen:** “I won't ask you to.”

**Q180–Q181:** The Seven freely contribute. The player defends mortal teams. Oth'varen completes the correction, sees the alignment hold, and physically dies. Sera calls the final inversion. Nhal's unified identity and memory shatter; his divine essence survives. No speculation on the fragments' destination.
