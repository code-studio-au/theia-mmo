# Theia Human 1–80 — Encounter Barks, Cinematic Beats and State Contract

**Status: Needs Review; original script and timing proposal, not engine-integrated.** [Canonical Human 1–80 campaign](Human_1_80_Canonical_Campaign.md) remains authoritative. [Branching key scenes](Human_1_80_Key_Scenes_Branching_Dialogue.md) contain the primary conversations. This companion describes short in-combat lines, scene transitions, conditional stage barks and accessibility requirements.

## Shared encounter dialogue rules

- Barks are short, interruptible, subtitled, and never the sole carrier of essential plot information. The quest journal and post-encounter dialogue contain all required facts.
- Timings are **relative to encounter state**, not fixed cinematic timestamps. Any numeric durations must be determined in playtests.
- No cinematic takes away control during active combat without a safe invulnerable or checkpointed transition. Classic-style manual target, facing and autoattack remain intact.
- Boss health thresholds below are **proposed trigger categories**, not final percentages. Avoid mandatory long invulnerability intermissions.
- Each bark fires at most once per encounter attempt unless marked retryable. Critical plot flags fire once per character; instance replay is noncanonical.
- Death, disconnect and solo-instance restart preserve prior evidence but reset combat phase state to the latest checkpoint.

## E01 — Q030 Hidden Passage: Sorn and Cassian

**Entry trigger:** player crosses the authorized dungeon threshold after civilian exits and evidence copies are secured.

**Mara, entry:** “The exits are clear. Secure the dispatches before Cassian can reach them.”

**Sorn, first aggro:** “The passage is closed. Turn back.”

**Sorn, first reinforcement call:** “Hold the archive door!”

**Mara, evidence checkpoint:** “The dispatches are copied. Whatever happens next, the record survives.”

**Sorn, low-health phase:** “Cassian! They're through!”

**Cassian, confronted:** “You think this ends with me?”

**Player response A:** “You're coming back alive.” → **Cassian:** “Then let the king hear what he wants to hear.”

**Player response B:** “Edric deserves the truth.” → **Cassian:** “Truth is a luxury for people who survive it.”

**Mara, arrest:** “Cassian, by the king's warrant, you are in custody.”

**Cutscene beats:** archive evidence secured → Sorn defeated → Cassian prevented from escaping → nonlethal arrest → return to Mara. No court execution or forced killing of Cassian.

## E02 — Q045 Silent Signal: Halvek and the Minor Resonator

**Sera, entry:** “The cutoff is behind the central console. Do not strike the core.”

**Halvek, aggro:** “You don't understand what the signal is for.”

**Halvek, control-phase call:** “Keep the channel open!”

**Sera, safety warning:** “The readings are rising. Isolate the support before touching the cutoff.”

**Halvek, defeat:** “The signal will find another route.”

**Sera, completion:** “The Minor Resonator is disabled. The measurements are intact.”

**Cutscene beats:** defeat Halvek → isolate supports → disable device → preserve independent readings. Failure before cutoff returns to a safe checkpoint.

## E03 — Q077 Against the Harbinger: Oma'vira

**Nessa, entry:** “Captives first. We can fight the Harbinger once the controls are down.”

**Oma'vira, first sight:** “You are far from the world that made you.”

**Oma'vira, after relay shutdown:** “You have freed them from one design. That does not free them from what is coming.”

**Sera, captive rescue:** “The control relays are isolated. Move the captives toward the marked exit.”

**Oma'vira, boss aggro:** “Then you will learn what interruption costs.”

**Oma'vira, final phase:** “The work does not end with me.”

**Sera, aftermath:** “Oma'vira is dead. Copy the gate records before we leave.”

**Cutscene beats:** control relay shutdown → captive evacuation → solo boss victory → permanent death → destroyed Passage gate evidence. No premature Nhal name or Kel'vari revelation.

## E04 — Q115 Witness Vault: Su'vareth

**Mara, entry:** “Every record here may be a lie. Compare it before you trust it.”

**Su'vareth, decoy revealed:** “A false trail is most useful when it leads somewhere believable.”

**Witness, rescue:** “I can speak, but get me out first.”

**Su'vareth, boss aggro:** “You have gathered two histories. I gave them both their certainty.”

**Su'vareth, final phase:** “They will continue the war without me.”

**Oth'varen, aftermath:** “She is dead. The orders she left behind may still be active.”

**Cutscene beats:** decoy identified → independent evidence confirmed → witnesses secured → Su'vareth killed → contingency orders copied. Do not imply the war automatically ends.

## E05 — Q145 The First Voice: Vaer'uun

**Vaer'uun, first sight:** “You came because I gave you a truth your companion withheld.”

**Player response A:** “That doesn't make you trustworthy.” → **Vaer'uun:** “No. It makes the record worth examining.”

**Player response B:** “You used us.” → **Vaer'uun:** “I knew you would follow evidence. I made use of that.”

**Sera, apparatus warning:** “The mechanism is responding to our research access. Shut it down.”

**Vaer'uun, after shutdown:** “You can stop this machine. You cannot restore the innocence of your gods.”

**Vaer'uun, final phase:** “I know whom I serve.”

**Sera, aftermath:** “Vaer'uun is dead. His evidence must still be judged on its own merits.”

**Cutscene beats:** truthful but manipulative argument → disable apparatus → defeat founder → preserve Keeper clue. Nhal's name is already available from Q143; Keeper's full deception is not yet established.

## E06 — Q156 Veilbreaker: Khor'vun

**Pre-entry instrument state:** threshold already passed. The player can inspect the reading, but cannot reverse it.

**Sera, approach:** “The sequence has crossed the cutoff. We still have to reach the chamber.”

**Khor'vun, aggro:** “You are too late to stop what has begun.”

**Khor'vun, mid-fight:** “The network no longer needs my hand.”

**Khor'vun, defeat:** “Then see what your victory has saved.”

**Player cutoff interaction:** mechanically completes the attempted intervention; it does **not** succeed. No skill-check or arbitrary player failure changes the outcome.

**Sera, world-state transition:** “The Veil is gone. The Binding has dissolved.”

**Tarek:** “The Shroud is withdrawing. It's begun already.”

**Oth'varen:** “The Seven are free. Nhal's presence will take time to gather. We must not waste that time.”

**Cutscene beats:** player defeats Khor'vun → attempts cutoff → Veil destroyed → Binding dissolves **immediately** → Seven freed immediately → Shroud starts dissipating → Nhal begins slow manifestation. No separate Binding-breaking machine, no instant full manifestation.

## E07 — Q176 The Architect's Last Argument: Thes'var

**Thes'var, first sight:** “You have reached the heart of a design you barely understand.”

**Player:** “You know what Nhal will do.”

**Thes'var:** “I know whom I serve. I know what I chose.”

**Thes'var, aggro:** “Then prove your answer can survive mine.”

**Thes'var, control interference:** “The alignment answers to me.”

**Thes'var, defeat:** “You cannot command what follows.”

**Sera, post-defeat inspection:** “Something has changed. The control sequence is no longer aligned.”

**Cutscene beats:** defeat and physically kill Thes'var → discover final sabotage afterward. Do not reveal a known fatal correction before the death.

## E08 — Q177–Q179 The Scholar's Correction and Choice

**Sera, initial inspection:** “The ordinary correction isn't holding. Each adjustment pushes another field out of phase.”

**Oth'varen, discovery:** “There is one correction left. It must be made inside the convergence.”

**Sera:** “That exposure will kill you.”

**Oth'varen:** “I know. I did not know when we entered.”

**Player A:** “There must be another way.” → **Oth'varen:** “We searched for one. We have no time left to search again.”

**Player B:** “I don't want you to die.” → **Oth'varen:** “Neither do I. That is why this is a choice, not a destiny.”

**Player C:** “Tell me what you need.” → **Oth'varen:** “Hold the stations. Protect the others. I will make the correction.”

**Sera:** “I won't call this fair.”

**Oth'varen:** “I won't ask you to.”

**Cutscene beats:** sabotage verified → ordinary correction fails → Oth'varen calculates lethal solution → tells Sera and player → player prepares defensive stations. No fake save-him branch.

## E09 — Q180–Q181 The Seven Hold / Shattering of Nhal

**Sera, start:** “The Seven are answering freely. The stations must remain stable.”

**Guardian technician, first threat:** “East station under attack!”

**Sera, correction begins:** “Oth'varen is entering the convergence. Hold your position.”

**Oth'varen, correction under strain:** “The fields are answering. Keep them aligned.”

**Guardian technician, stabilization:** “All stations holding!”

**Oth'varen, final:** “I can see it. The alignment holds. Finish it.”

**Scene order:** Oth'varen completes correction → visually verifies alignment → physically dies → Sera directs final inversion → player completes final defense/activation → Nhal's coherent identity, memory and unified will shatter; divine essence persists fragmented.

**Sera, confirmation:** “Nhal's will is gone. His essence remains, but it no longer holds together as the being we fought.”

**Important:** No fragment destination, reincarnation, new antagonist, or future divine identity is established in the launch ending.

## E10 — Q182–Q184 Aftermath and Homecoming

**Sera, memorial:** “Oth'varen knew the cost only when the last choice was before him. He made it, and he did not come back.”

**Mara, report:** “The Shroud began fading when Veilbreaker destroyed the Veil. That history must be recorded correctly.”

**Corven:** “Nhal is defeated. Our nations still have to choose what comes next.”

**Elara, homecoming:** “I sent you out to learn what the village needed. You found a world that needed more. I'm glad you found the road home.”

**Cutscene beats:** survivors and Seven confirmed → Oth'varen memorial → separate Covenant/Kel'vari reports → unresolved war → personal Millhaven closure.
