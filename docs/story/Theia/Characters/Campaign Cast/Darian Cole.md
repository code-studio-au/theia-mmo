Darian remains in the human military on temporary assignment to Commander Nessa's integrated Northwatch garrison, serving in supply, rescue and extraction rather than becoming an elite battlefield champion. His arc moves from enthusiastic Millhaven recruit to dependable soldier and eventual mentor to new recruits. He survives the launch campaign. Human players retain their Q002/Q033 history with him; other races meet him as a Northwatch soldier. He is unrelated to Brenna Holt.
