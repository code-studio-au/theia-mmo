## Identity and terrain
**CANONICAL:** The Human level 30–35 campaign places the Chainworks on the mainland, before the restricted Shroud crossing (Q063–Q079). The [Unmade](../../Lore/Institutions/The%20Unmade.md) facility and Anchor operations follow that campaign sequence.

**SUPERSEDED:** The historical outer-Shroud placement does not describe the current Chainworks.

## Connections
**OPEN:** Exact mainland approaches are not mapped here. The historical expedition track from Lantern Redoubt is **SUPERSEDED** as a current route to the Chainworks; it belonged to the older outer-Shroud placement. Overseas external-link investigations remain historical proposal context, not an approved map connection.

## Community and function
**ARCHIVED design detail:** Fortified operation adapted over generations, local supports and limited gate/courier supplies. These source details do not establish a surviving gate at this site. The World Bible's prohibition on army-scale Passage transfer remains in force.
