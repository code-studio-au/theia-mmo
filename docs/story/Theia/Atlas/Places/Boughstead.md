# Boughstead

**Status:** First Hunt campaign placement approved for further work. Settlement layout, distances, map coordinates and placed services are working details.

## Identity and terrain

Orchard village, cultivated glades, a lower cottage lane and the east footpath. Put the healer, ward yard, repair bench and provisions on accessible village routes.

## People and services

Talan, Maerin, Neris, Nivelle and Faerun.

## Quest route and aftermath

Main quests AE1-01 through AE1-06. The first cub goes into Nivelle’s care. Mark the safe footpath before the family takes the road to Ridgewatch.
