## The First Hunt · levels 14–20

Postwar Aelari woodland capital. Stage the outer watch post and timber yards below the canopy; place the market gate, clinic, lower walkways and nursery on usable city routes. The outer orchards adjoin the old woodland crossing and homes below it.

### People and services

Edrin, Sireth, Avariel and Ilyen; Lethiel and Rennel are working names.

### Quest route and aftermath

Main quests AE1-12 through AE1-18. Keep the residential branch inside the revised ward and leave a woodland route. Stage the reunion in a short solo encounter. The hall hosts the final meal; city services and side chains are available independently.
