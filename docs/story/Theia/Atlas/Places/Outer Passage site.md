## Community and function
**WORKING site detail:** Continuously weak physical-space connection; throughput and extraction govern the small expedition.

**CANONICAL distinction:** Oma'vira's gate is destroyed (Q079). Q081–Q085 concern a separate surviving Guardian gate with restricted capacity and custodial/Kel'vari permission. The World Bible confirms that some Passage connections remained continuously operational at low capacity, not that every old transit site survived.
