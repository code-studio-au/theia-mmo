## Level 1–20 zone brief

**Placement status:** Campaign routes and named quest sites follow the relevant campaign. New spatial arrangements and service locations are working proposals.

### Terrain and settlement

The inland human capital, with market, artisan, scholarly, military, royal and transport quarters. The settlement register gives each quarter its civic and service function.

### Connections and field routes

The Stoneford road reaches the city before the formal audience. Keep normal access to market and training separate from royal investigation access. The royal road connects toward Tidehold, and the disused administrative passage is the finale site.

### Daily life and player services

Markets and banking, specialist crafting, archives and magic training, military suppliers, royal administration and regional travel support serve different visitors. Mara works from an investigative office. Aldren’s court and Garrick’s headquarters are distinct destinations.

### Quest points of interest

Corroborate the records before the audience, follow Garrick’s lead, and use the Hidden Passage to reach Cassian. The public index, ration dispute and approach-road patrol give the player reasons to use the city beyond court.
