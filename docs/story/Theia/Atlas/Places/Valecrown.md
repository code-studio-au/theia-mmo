## Level 1–20 zone brief

**Placement status:** Campaign routes and named quest sites follow the relevant campaign. New spatial arrangements and service locations are working proposals.

### Terrain and settlement

Valecrown is the inland human capital. The market and artisan quarters support everyday commerce. The scholarly quarter houses archives and instruction, the military quarter serves the garrison, and the royal quarter houses the court and offices. A transport district connects the city to regional roads. The royal court and the Covenant’s alliance government have separate seats.

### Connections and field routes

The Stoneford road reaches the city before the player earns a formal audience. Players can reach markets, workshops and training through ordinary streets, while the investigation leads from Mara’s office to the records and royal audience. The disused administrative passage is the separate finale site. The royal road leads toward Tidehold.

### Daily life and player services

Markets supply provisions, banking and trade. Artisan workshops handle specialised equipment and training, the scholarly quarter supports research and magical instruction, and Garrick works from military headquarters. Mara receives evidence in her investigative office and Aldren holds audiences at court. Exact transport facilities and service placements remain working details.

### Quest points of interest

Verify the courier and warehouse testimony, consult the records, and take the corroborated case to Aldren. The pursuit reaches Cassian through the Hidden Passage. The damaged public index, the barracks ration shortage and the approach-road patrol give the player reasons to visit other quarters while the investigation is underway.
