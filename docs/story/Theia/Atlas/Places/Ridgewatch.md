# Ridgewatch

**Status:** First Hunt campaign placement approved for further work. Settlement layout, distances, map coordinates and placed services are working details.

## Identity and terrain

Trail town with a game hall, infirmary, inn and outfitter. Provide an upper ridge approach to the wolf den and a lower route to the wrecked camp. The hauling road continues toward Sylvaren.

## People and services

Rhael, Thaelis, Saela and Veyrien; Talan and Faerun return.

## Quest route and aftermath

Main quests AE1-07 through AE1-11. The second cub stays with Thaelis. The cage latch and Veyrien’s account send the player to the outer watch post.
