# Lantern Redoubt

**Authority:** [World Bible](../../../THEIA_WORLD_BIBLE.md) for world constraints; [canonical Human campaign](../../Campaigns/Human_1_80_Canonical_Campaign.md) for the established launch location and access sequence. Historical source details below remain WORKING or ARCHIVED unless explicitly established there.

> [!note] Working geography
> Exact distances, bearings and travel times remain OPEN. Historical route proposals do not override established campaign geography or access.

## Identity and terrain
**CANONICAL:** A longstanding restricted Guardian–Kel'vari outpost, not a newly founded Covenant reconnaissance post. See the canonical campaign's non-negotiable rules and Q080–Q088.

## Connections
**CANONICAL:** Q085 crosses the surviving low-capacity Guardian Passage with custodial permission and registers at Lantern Redoubt; Q084 separately requires Kel'vari approval. Restricted local access does not imply unrestricted travel through the Shroud.

**OPEN:** The historical “outer site” label is not an approved identification of a particular gate or mapped approach.

## Community and function
**WORKING historical proposal:** Small leased/permitted ruin near Thuma'rei-linked custodians. The precise site, lease and adjacency are not established by the campaign and must not be inferred from the related-note link.

**CANONICAL boundary:** Guardian–Kel'vari custodial access and independent Kel'vari consent do not authorize Covenant annexation.
