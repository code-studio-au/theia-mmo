# Travel and Transport

Theia's initial transport options are **ships and flight paths**, connecting a limited network of established hubs. Journeys take place within the world and follow its geography and political access. Players continue on foot or by mount from the arrival hub to villages, wilderness objectives, ruins and story locations.

Flight paths follow fixed routes; they do not grant unrestricted player-controlled flight. Discovering routes through exploration and learning the land should be valuable. Mount unlock levels, flight carriers, hub locations, route-discovery rules, fares, schedules and travel durations remain open.

Travel creates opportunities for roadside encounters, local stories and exploration. Not every journey requires an encounter. Regional quests should combine useful objectives into worthwhile expeditions and avoid repeatedly sending the player over the same long route for a single conversation.

The ordinary transport network does not provide instant teleportation between discovered waypoints. Ancient Passage gates remain rare, significant story connections with their established capacity limits. They do not become a universal fast-travel service. Coaches in earlier draft material are not selected for the initial transport network; later additions and any limited recall abilities require separate development.

This creator lock specifies travel design, not implemented gameplay or approval of the proposed level-80 cap.

Creator approval: on 31 October 2026, the creator explicitly locked physical in-world travel with ships and flight paths as the initial transport options. This note records that scoped decision directly.
