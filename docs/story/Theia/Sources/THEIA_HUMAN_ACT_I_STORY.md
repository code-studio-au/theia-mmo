# THEIA — HUMAN ACT I: THE CROWN AND THE BETRAYAL

**Status: creator-approved and locked (15 October 2026).** This document preserves the human racial Act I narrative, levels 1–20. Exact zone and settlement names, individual quests, combat balance, dialogue, dungeon mechanics and Cassian's final legal disposition remain open. The campaign must be solo-accessible and permit independent levelling.

## Narrative premise

The player begins as an ordinary human adventurer in an agricultural district of the inland human kingdom, far from the royal court. The continuing war with [[Dominion]] affects taxes, recruitment, transport and families, but the area is not an active battlefield. The player's first tasks concern local people and credible local threats, not a prophecy or an obvious [[The Unmade|Unmade]] conspiracy.

**Captain [[Elara Fenwick]]**, the district's respected militia commander, becomes the player's first mentor. As the player proves reliable, a series of ordinary thefts reveals a supply-diversion scheme reaching into royal administration. That discovery carries the player to the human racial capital, where **[[Mara Thorne]]** investigates and **[[King Aldren Vaelmont]]** recounts his sincerely held understanding of the [[Shattered Delegation]]. The helpful **[[Lord Cassian Veyr]]**, the King's new First Royal Counsellor, secretly attempts to redirect the inquiry because he helped compromise the delegation to arrange the death of **[[Lord Edric Halewyn]]**, his predecessor.

The Act I climax exposes Cassian through corroborated evidence and the King's lawful authority. It does **not** reveal the full Unmade conspiracy, Nhal's identity, or definitively absolve Dominion.

## Chapter I — Trouble in the Heartlands (approximately levels 1–5)

The starting district contains farms, villages, trade roads, woodland, river crossings and ordinary wildlife. War has increased financial and recruitment pressures without making every problem a military conspiracy. Players can hunt neutral fauna, fight natural predators, gather materials and resolve local difficulties independently of the main story.

Elara asks the player to help with farm threats, a dangerous road and a missing merchant caravan. Human outlaws near an abandoned roadside settlement are suspected of ordinary banditry. Their thefts prove selective: they take certain tools, metal fittings, sealed containers and correspondence while leaving other valuables. Most outlaws know only that an unidentified buyer pays for particular goods.

The player recovers part of the shipment and a damaged document bearing a royal administrative seal. Elara recognises its significance but cannot verify its provenance. She secures it rather than declaring a conspiracy.

**Outcome:** Elara trusts the player; the first clue connects a local crime to an unknown purchaser.

## Chapter II — The Road to the Capital (approximately levels 5–10)

The thefts continue after the first outlaw camp is disrupted. Elara sends the player to neighbouring settlements, storage sites and merchant routes to investigate the larger operation. Local stories reveal real wartime pressures: grain requisitioning, transport disputes, retired soldiers, ordinary criminals and dangerous wilderness. These problems are not all Unmade plots.

The player traces diverted shipments to a regional merchant and discovers records of payments through intermediaries using authorisations associated with royal administration. One seal appears connected to an office reorganised after the Shattered Delegation. Elara lacks authority to authenticate it and gives the player a letter of introduction to senior Royal Investigator Mara Thorne in the inland capital.

**Outcome:** A credible reason to visit the capital, not a summons based merely on the player's level.

## Chapter III — The King's Confidence (approximately levels 10–15)

The human racial capital introduces markets, barracks, scholars, royal institutions, professions and independent side quests. Mara initially doubts the evidence and requires corroboration through administrative registers, merchants and warehouse records.

The records lead towards credentials associated with the former First Royal Counsellor's office. **Lord Edric Halewyn** held that office until he died during the Shattered Delegation. Mara secures a royal audience.

King Aldren explains Edric's personal importance, the effort to negotiate peace and the devastating attack, including the evidence he believes implicates Dominion. He does not know the account was manipulated. Cassian is present, treats the player respectfully and offers plausible assistance. He suggests a Dominion intelligence operation and grants access to records while quietly steering the investigation towards false leads.

The King authorises Mara and the player to continue. The player initially has good reasons to trust Cassian.

**Outcome:** Royal access, personal understanding of the King's grief, and an apparently helpful adviser whose misdirection begins to shape the investigation.

## Chapter IV — The Betrayal Within (approximately levels 15–20)

Independent verification reveals contradictions: a shipment was authorised before the alleged Dominion request; a witness's identification was suggested rather than spontaneous; and a document attributed to Edric uses administrative details introduced only after his death.

Mara avoids premature accusation. The player recovers evidence that Cassian accessed the delegation's security arrangements and supplied them to intermediaries who promised to kill Edric. Cassian expected a targeted assassination and political advancement, not the devastation of the whole gathering. After succeeding Edric, he remained under the intermediaries' leverage. The player still cannot identify the full Unmade leadership or prove the wider conspiracy.

Mara arranges a private audience with Aldren. Cassian calls the evidence Dominion forgery, but independent corroboration establishes his complicity. Aldren orders him detained pending lawful investigation. Cassian attempts to flee through a route prepared by his contacts.

### Act I solo story dungeon — The Hidden Passage (working name)

A disused passage and storage network beneath an old administrative complex near the capital has been adapted for covert transport. Mara, royal guards and the player pursue Cassian through smugglers, hired fighters and a few unusually disciplined operatives associated with the Veiled Hand's network. Their actual allegiance remains undisclosed.

At the deepest chamber Cassian discovers his contacts intend to silence him, not rescue him. The player defeats the operatives and can bring Cassian into royal custody. **Capturing him alive is the preferred story direction**, but his exact fate and mechanics remain open. The dungeon is completable solo with appropriate NPC assistance; group modes may add challenge and rewards without withholding essential story.

### Aftermath

Aldren confronts the betrayal of a trusted adviser and orders further inquiry. Cassian's guilt does not prove Dominion's innocence in the Shattered Delegation, end the war or reveal [[Nhal]]. The player receives a formal commission to continue helping Mara, naturally leading into Act II.

## Character development

- **Elara Fenwick:** From local mentor to continuing link with the home district and the effects of kingdom-wide decisions.
- **Mara Thorne:** From sceptical investigator to dependable ally who insists on corroboration.
- **King Aldren Vaelmont:** From grieving ruler convinced of Dominion treachery to a monarch forced to recognise betrayal within his own court.
- **Lord Cassian Veyr:** From competent and seemingly helpful adviser to exposed collaborator, not an omniscient cult leader.
- **Lord Edric Halewyn:** Present through records, former colleagues and his legacy of principled counsel.
- **[[Garrick Ashford]]:** A possible early suspect because he opposed peace, but innocent of the attack and potentially a military ally after the evidence is established.

## Continuation through Acts II–V

**Act II:** Mara traces Cassian's intermediaries while Garrick introduces overseas military operations. **Act III:** Evidence suggests a conspiracy extending beyond the human kingdom, complicating relations with [[Covenant]] institutions. **Act IV:** Aldren and High Ruler [[Corven Vaelmont]] confront different responsibilities in preparing for the Shroud. **Act V:** The Anchor network's true danger challenges assumptions about the war and what the kingdom has failed to recognise.

## Guardrails

1. Do not make the player a chosen saviour or Guardian initiate in Act I.
2. Keep the human King and Covenant High Ruler, and their capitals, separate.
3. Maintain geographically credible wildlife and human local criminals; not every encounter serves the Unmade.
4. The new adviser is knowingly culpable but does not know Nhal's full purpose.
5. Establish guilt through corroborated evidence and lawful royal authority.
6. Do not prematurely reveal the Conclave or settle the Shattered Delegation's full authorship in the player's knowledge.
7. Preserve independent levelling, exploration and side quests.
8. Do not require raids or PvP for story completion.
