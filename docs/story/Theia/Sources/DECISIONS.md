# Story decisions and maintenance

## 2026-10-26 — Story home moves to Theia-MMO

The user chose to continue game development in this repository and requested a
dedicated subfolder for Theia's story, lore, characters, acts, quests and story
decisions. `docs/story/` is that home. The Engine remains a research/reference
source; the transfer preserves its original story material without deleting it.

This is a repository ownership decision, not a new canon approval. Existing
creator locks, rejected directions, superseded choices and open questions retain
their recorded status. In particular, the complete human campaign review packet
remains Needs Review.

## Existing story decisions

The imported [Source Decision Recovery](THEIA_SOURCE_DECISION_RECOVERY.md) and

## Future updates

- Record new explicit creator decisions here with date, scope, approval evidence
  and affected documents; retain any superseded decision as history.
- Update the affected narrative documents so this index and their status agree.
- Keep proposals labelled WORKING or Needs Review until explicitly accepted.
- Keep story truth separate from when characters and players learn it.
- Keep design status separate from implemented behavior and runtime evidence.
- Keep private raw conversation/account exports out of Git.
- Treat the import manifest as the immutable transfer baseline. Subsequent edits
  should have their own decision or review record rather than rewriting its hashes.
