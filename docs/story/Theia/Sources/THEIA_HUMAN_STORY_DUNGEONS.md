# THEIA — HUMAN SOLO STORY CLIMAXES

**Status: Needs Review.** Narrative encounter designs for the [Human Campaign](THEIA_HUMAN_CAMPAIGN.md) and [Quest Register](THEIA_HUMAN_QUEST_REGISTER.md). None is implemented, balanced or playtested. Five story versions carry the entire essential plot; optional group/raid variants may add challenge, equipment and supplementary lore only.

## Common encounter contract

Every story version supports a solo martial or magical character without a specific profession, consumable purchase, companion build or PvP result. NPCs provide specialist work appropriate to their knowledge. The player still controls the route, immediate threats and decisive interaction. No NPC simply wins the encounter while the player watches.

The environment uses static authored passages and chambers. Disable/repair verbs alter gameplay objects, service state or visual effects, not terrain collision or navigation-changing doors. Set pieces use existing exits rather than mandatory building destruction.

Checkpoints retain case progress and restore companions needed for a retry. Failure never erases the only clue, permanently kills a principal witness or requires a live world-event state to recover. Captures occur through an authored defeat/surrender scene; they do not require a chosen nonlethal class skill. Story investigators may retrieve evidence before or after combat credit, with safe copies covering disrupted interactions.

Finite NPC reserves and task boundaries matter. Specialist actions are distinct individual workings, not ordinary cooperative multi-mage casting. Class-independent tools are quest equipment usable for that scene, not permanent mastery of six magical disciplines.

## Act I — The Hidden Passage

**Entry:** H1-17 after Aldren's lawful detention order. **Site:** disused storage and transport passages below an administrative complex near [[Valecrown]]. **Allies:** Mara, a small royal guard detail. **Principal opponents:** human smugglers, cleanup operatives and [[Varric Sorn]]. **Story goal:** capture Cassian alive and preserve the route to his intermediaries.

1. **The abandoned loading room.** The player secures the entry while Mara checks a registry case and guards hold the already open retreat. Smugglers fight to protect goods and escape, not because every participant knows the ancient cult. An optional surrender interaction adds testimony but never the only clue.
2. **The witness alcove.** The player finds Cassian's frightened courier and checks a ledger copy against the route mark. The apparent time pressure resolves as a checkpointed scene. Mara escorts the courier to a safe existing side passage; an accompanying guard stays available to the player.
3. **The cleanup station.** Sorn's team burns disposable copies while a dispatch duplicate sits in a protected lockbox. The player interrupts a courier, secures the box and stops Sorn's escape. The antagonist fights with disciplined hired guards, without revealing a Conclave emblem or [[Nhal]] monologue.
4. **The man they will not rescue.** Cassian discovers the cleanup team intends to silence him. Sorn is defeated and detained. The player can protect Cassian without excusing him; a brief chase through an existing passage ends in surrender to Mara and the royal guards. Essential testimony can be taken again after a retry.

**Player-facing scene:** Cassian says, “They promised one death.” Mara answers, “You bought it. The promise does not make you innocent.” The player chooses whether to ask about his ambition, the security leak or the surviving contacts; all three answers remain available in the case summary.

**Exit state:** Cassian alive in lawful custody, removed from office; Sorn detained; the dispatch trail preserved. The King authorises an inquiry. The player knows a wider network exists, not who ultimately commands it. Reward: human service recognition and a choice of ordinary equipment appropriate to the eventual item system.

## Act II — The Silent Signal

**Entry:** H2-15 after a documented cutoff plan. **Site:** an abandoned [[Dominion]] coastal signal house adapted by a covert cell. **Allies:** Sera and Brynja; limited written guidance from the Grey Surveyor. **Principal opponent:** [[Halvek Dorn]]. **Story goal:** disable a Minor Resonator and recover its observed design.

1. **A building with more than one occupant.** The player secures the adapted service passage. Ordinary cave fauna in side spaces are not all corrupted. A clear warning marks the route into the active installation. Brynja checks that the mechanical isolator matches the inspected housing.
2. **Separate the controls.** The player moves a physical selector to isolate the local feed and defends Brynja while she locks its housing. Sera handles a separate access point. These are sequential, independent tasks; the player is not asked to stand on three pressure plates simultaneously.
3. **The specimen cage.** A bound defender can be released by disabling its local conditioning link after the adjacent attackers are subdued. Others remain dangerously hostile; the game does not label combat against an immediate threat a moral failure. The sample preservation/destruction choice was resolved in H2-13, and the measurement record exists either way.
4. **A useful silence.** Halvek attempts to restore the device. The player prevents access to its exposed control and defeats him. Brynja completes the isolation; the player executes the documented cutoff. Its effects stop locally, while the report distinguishes that observation from a cure for the worldwide awakening.

**Player-facing scene:** Brynja says, “I can tell you what stopped. I cannot yet tell you what else it was reaching.” Sera thanks the player for protecting both people and evidence without disclosing every Guardian secret.

**Exit state:** Halvek detained, device disabled, evidence copied, feasible captives secured. The surrounding Dominion military still opposes [[Covenant]]. The independent garrison defence in H2-16 supplies the act's military consequence through an NPC story route if no live event is active.

## Act III — The Broken Measure

**Entry:** H3-16 after the player interrupts the cell's incomplete destruction procedure. **Site:** old freight works below the [[Korrath]] expedition approach, repurposed around a Regional Anchor. **Allies:** Sera, Halden's recovery team and the Grey Surveyor. **Principal opponents:** a willing local [[The Unmade|Unmade]] cell and conditioned defenders. **Story goal:** save the exposed team and isolate the linked installation safely.

1. **The warning proved.** A surge demonstrates that removing one support while its neighbours remain connected is dangerous. The player uses a marked emergency isolator to protect the recovery team. Halden hears the omitted measurement, sees its effect and changes orders; the dispute is resolved by evidence rather than humiliating him as a fool.
2. **Three support stations.** Each station has a readable local task: secure an access lever, recover a calibration plate, then lock an external feed. Qualified team members perform their separate observations. The player chooses the next station from the safe sequence explained in the briefing. An unsafe attempt produces a warning and recoverable local reset, not an untelegraphed campaign-ending failure.
3. **Evacuate before pursuit.** The cell's overseer attempts to remove design records while bound guards attack the retreat. The essential case has a second copy; the player rescues the threatened team first and defeats or drives back the overseer's guard. Optional pursuit can yield additional nonessential notes.
4. **One core, not one world.** With the external links isolated, the Surveyor performs an individual combined stability working. The player holds the cutoff against a final attack and confirms the readings. The core is safely decommissioned. A separate old Passage route survives because physical transit and Anchor resonance are different systems.

**Player-facing scene:** Halden says, “The instruction was mine to give.” The Surveyor answers, “And the missing page was ours to keep. We will not keep it from you again.” Their practical correction is distributed safety information, not instant ideological agreement.

**Exit state:** the regional operation stopped, endangered allies rescued, copies made and a small-party Passage survey made possible. The player's associate role is recognised. The Surveyor's unusual skill prompts questions but his Sixth Scholar identity remains for Act IV.

## Act IV — The Witness Vault

**Entry:** H4-15 after storm-coast records point to the station and separate captive exits are surveyed. **Site:** an Unmade research outpost in the outer Shroud, away from protected enclave interiors. **Allies:** Sera, [[Oth'varen]] and a limited rescue party. **Principal opponents:** [[Teren Malrec]] and local conditioned forces; [[Oma'vira]] intervenes through a prepared installation. **Story goal:** rescue accessible captives and secure independent Delegation evidence.

1. **The captive route.** The player secures a transit room and disables a local restraint device. Noncombatants exit through a guarded existing passage. Opposing-alliance captives, if included in later production, receive no friendly quest state or shared military command; neutral civilian survivors are the default draft population.
2. **The copied transaction.** Sera isolates archive storage while the player fights through the defended outer workspace. Records include dates, access instructions and false-attribution material; none contains a complete divine history. A duplicate case credit survives resetting the encounter.
3. **The Harbinger's claim.** Malrec activates a prepared Mind/Form focus. The proposed projection operates only through this local installation and can be interrupted at marked anchors; it is not an unlimited remote possession power. Oma'vira recognises Oth'varen and provokes his persistent corruption. He withdraws from the active focus, warns the team and asks the player to use the isolation procedure already taught.
4. **A choice still resisted.** The player disables the focus's local support, protects retreating captives and defeats Malrec. Oth'varen chooses resistance through his own effort; the player removes a source of pressure, not the corruption within him. The projection ends. Oma'vira is neither killed nor redeemed.

**Player-facing scene:** Oth'varen says, “Do not take my certainty for proof. Take the record. Check it.” This earns trust by giving the player a means to question him. It does not make their trust compulsory.

**Exit state:** Malrec detained, conditioning site disabled, accessible captives extracted, transaction archive secured. Mara's later comparison supplies the political conclusion. Ending a combat encounter is not automatically proof that every document it contained is genuine.

## Act V — The Severed Chain

**Entry:** H5-14–H5-16, after the four allied peoples' contributions, fourth overseas expedition and isolated inversion trial. **Site:** [[Chainworks]] Major Anchor installation, outer Shroud. **Allies:** Nessa's Covenant expedition, Garrick's human contribution, Oth'varen and cleared technical staff under enclave boundary agreements. **Principal local opponents:** Voss and [[Sareth Keld]]. **Founder involvement:** [[Thes'var]] directly, Khor'vun's defensive doctrine, Vaer'uun's operational directive. **Story goal:** prevent this branch's expansion and extract the team, with a bounded procedure choice.

1. **Before the assault is glory.** The player evacuates coerced workers and cuts the outer courier link. Darian leads an NPC extraction detail. Aru'ven's permitted boundary stays clear. Civilian rescue is essential staging, not a loot detour after a boss kill.
2. **The last broker.** Voss tries to destroy timing evidence at a relay station. The player secures its lodged copy, defeats her guards and captures her. Mara's chain of custody remains distinct from Garrick's military objective.
3. **The Warden's lesson.** Keld's defence uses three separate support stations and reinforcements. The player isolates them one by one while NPCs hold secured stations. Reinforcement links become unavailable because their gameplay objects are disabled, not because a building collapses. The player defeats and detains Keld, earning access to the synchronising chamber through an existing passage.
4. **A disagreement between enemies.** Vaer'uun's directive prioritises the synchronising core; Thes'var's emergency adjustments sacrifice part of it to preserve his research. The conflict gives the player a window to stop forced synchronisation. Thes'var remains an active danger and uses a visible prepared withdrawal route once his immediate objective is lost. The founder's ultimate fate is not assigned.
5. **The player commits the procedure.** Oth'varen identifies the combined working he can perform alone. The player controls mechanical isolation, fights immediate attackers and chooses safe severance or bounded inversion. On severance, decommissioning ends the local strain. On inversion, a short documented counter-resonance is tested; reaching the stated limit triggers deliberate severance. If conditions exceed safe bounds, the fallback completes the severance branch. Neither outcome promises a permanent inverted Major Anchor.
6. **Everyone accounted for.** The player assists the last extraction under Nessa's command and brings the report out. Darian survives through preparation and teamwork. The optional additional group encounter may follow remaining defenders but contains no sole proof of the story's success.

**Player-facing scene:** Oth'varen hands over the full cutoff sequence: “You have the readings. If I ask for more than they permit, stop me.” The player's final choice has practical authority rather than being a dialogue selection after an NPC has already solved everything.

**Exit state shared by both branches:** installation isolated, expansion stopped, local expedition approaches protected, captives extracted, critical evidence preserved, founders alive, broader network extant, Nhal still confined and intermittently communicative elsewhere. Detailed branch measurements feed H5-17; political, Guardian and home epilogues complete H5-18–H5-20.

## Optional group variants

Group modes can add extra defence stations, more simultaneous combat roles, optional elite commanders and supplementary archive rooms. These require separate balance and implementation work. They cannot be the exclusive source of Cassian's capture, Unmade attribution, Oth'varen's identity, the inversion hypothesis, the launch victory or the home ending. No raid kill is inserted into a principal quest's prerequisites.
