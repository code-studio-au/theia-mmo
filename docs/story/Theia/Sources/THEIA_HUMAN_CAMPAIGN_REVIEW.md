# THEIA — HUMAN CAMPAIGN REVIEW RECORD

**Status: Needs Review.** Prepared 15 October 2026. The complete proposed human launch story is ready for creator review; none of its new connective lore is labelled approved or locked. This record describes narrative and document validation, not a playable game build.

## Deliverable and scope

The packet contains a five-act level 1–60 narrative with 21 chapters including the homecoming; 92 principal quests; 24 optional local quests; five solo story climaxes; a cast, geography and services register; and connected material for every other playable people that appears in the route. It also supplies 12 proposed Dominion-side companion quests for the four native peoples' own future campaign writers. Those 12 are not part of the human player's 92 principal quests.

The human launch arc reaches a defined ending. Cassian is captured, removed, tried and sentenced in the proposed outcome; the wider sabotage is independently established; Aldren corrects his own account; a local Major Anchor expansion is stopped; recurring humans receive concluding scenes; the player returns home. No later expansion is required to receive that ending. The other seven peoples' complete 1–60 stories and the ultimate cosmic saga are outside this packet.

Start with [Human Campaign](THEIA_HUMAN_CAMPAIGN.md). Use the [Quest Register](THEIA_HUMAN_QUEST_REGISTER.md) for progression, [Solo Story Climaxes](THEIA_HUMAN_STORY_DUNGEONS.md) for encounters, [Cast and Places](THEIA_HUMAN_CAST_AND_PLACES.md) for availability, and [Connected Racial Stories](THEIA_HUMAN_CONNECTED_RACIAL_STORIES.md) for sovereign and opposing perspectives.
