## Narrative rewrite and historical causation

The [current interwoven chronicle](THEIA_INTERWOVEN_CHRONICLE.md) was substantially expanded and reordered to keep related histories together rather than isolate gods, races, hidden orders and modern politics in disconnected entries. Its continuous causal sequence now covers:

- Aethyr's **deliberate desperate First Sundering** and the Seven's independent awakening; the gods' mixed morality, emerging friendships, rivalries, creative philosophies and relationships to mortals.
- The six species, Keeper and Ascendant's cooperation/distrust during troll creation, divine empowerment of mortal bloodlines and the growth of complex independent magical institutions.
- The **three-or-four-order** Age of Wonders, knowledge sharing, Passage infrastructure and the social dependencies that made its destruction devastating.
- Ascendant's **sole** Seventh creation using Keeper's restricted knowledge beyond permission, Nhal's later influence only after the Seventh researchers' accidental resonance, the seventh civilization's First Anchor and the initial Second Sundering catastrophe, followed by the Divine Schism and War.
- Keeper's investigation of the intelligence outside creation and **The Binding**, in which Keeper knowingly conceals the indefinite binding of all Seven. The moral betrayer and the gods' survival are now locked; wartime sides and the exact magical mechanism remain open.
- Guardians founded during Divine War; The Unmade rooted in Nhal-influenced ancient research; the **working** loss of Unmade contact after the sealing and renewed contact during the awakening.
- Distinct postwar survival of the dwarf and Korrath ancestral capitals, destruction of the original elven capital, migrations of all six races, halfling refuge among orcs, Covenant's human–dwarf treaty and Dominion's trial-based leadership.
- Aelari–Vaelari responsibility schism and Korrath–Vorrath religious exile, including Queen Seralyth, First Archon Selrion and First Elder Dromak; other proposed modern rulers remain marked as working names.
- Generations of resource warfare and naval expeditions, exceptional peace negotiations sabotaged by The Unmade, Guardian investigation and the expansion of hostile overseas outposts.
- Level-banded military, Guardian and Unmade incursions on both continents, the later Shrouded Lands PvP theatre and two permanently separate launch campaigns toward a working level-60 cap.

## Later creator clarification: the deliberate two-realm First Sundering (2026-10-12)

The user supplied an expanded **draft**, not final prose, explaining **why** Aethyr shattered itself. Aethyr and Nhal were supreme primordial essences before space, time, laws or realms. Their conflict of creation versus unmaking reached a stalemate. Aethyr realized that **imprisonment through separation**, rather than direct defeat, could protect future creation. This replaces earlier ambiguity about whether the Void existed before creation or the Veil was incidental. ## 2026-10-12 confirmed troll-creation revision

The user explicitly rejected an earlier narrative draft that made trolls sound nearly unkillable and approved a different explanation for Ascendant's unauthorized modification. **Ascendant amplified the trolls' ancestral connection to divine essence**, hoping to be worshipped over other gods. The alteration helps explain the trolls' unusually strong religious traditions, while preserving their free will and mortality; it does not grant exceptional regeneration, instant evolution, guaranteed divine communication, superior magical reserves or combat power. Keeper's objection is an ethical and trust breach, not evidence that trolls are defective. Troll devotion later divides between Keeper and Ascendant, contributing to—but not mechanically causing—the Korrath–Vorrath religious conflict and exile. The old automatic-adaptation narrative is superseded. The World Bible, Detailed Lore Archive and Interwoven Chronicle have been revised accordingly.

## 2026-10-12 canon lock: Guardians' founding and deliberate Seventh secrecy

The user approved the full Guardian origin developed after the unique Seventh cross-species rule. **During the Divine War, before The Binding**, surviving Seventh investigators—some already abroad when the First Anchor activated, others escaping before the Shroud—formed a **multiracial secret coalition** with trusted scholars, witnesses and protectors of the other six races. They knew only that **an unknown influence was manipulating magical research**, not Nhal's identity. They preserved evidence, sought to prevent repeat catastrophes, and refused to entrust the whole truth to a single god or mortal institution.

The Guardians established cells and hidden repositories on both sides of what would become the Shroud. After The Binding, weakened but continuously low-capacity gates allowed limited contact, not mass evacuation. **Early Guardians intentionally concealed surviving Seventh enclaves** to protect them. Compartmentalization, lost archives, migration and intergenerational secrecy gradually caused **many later Guardians to forget the full truth themselves**. The Guardians are neither all Seventh nor hereditary; their recruits come from the six races and possibly unrecognized Seventh descendants. Their growing secrecy creates a thematic parallel with Keeper's withheld truth.

This resolves the earlier *proposed* Guardian ancestry and concealment as **confirmed user decisions**. The Seventh's small enclaves, the Unmade's Nhal-serving movement, the Guardians' multiracial mission and the separate secretly surviving original great magical order remain distinct. Exact founders, archives, senior knowledge and internal offices are still open. Updated files: World Bible, Interwoven Chronicle, Detailed Lore Archive and Source Decision Recovery.

## Later 2026-10-12 creator lock — observation without interaction; creation without corruption

The creator explicitly rejected the previously recorded Q40 causal interpretation of Nhal secretly influencing the seventh race **during Ascendant's creation**. The historical source answer is preserved in the original-question register, but this **later direct decision takes precedence**. Ascendant independently created the seventh race through persistent ambition, ingenuity and the desire to surpass the earlier six races, granting exceptional aptitude for combining all six magical fields. **Nhal did not alter, empower, guide or corrupt Ascendant's design.**

Nhal remained imprisoned behind the Veil but could **observe** the Physical Realm, its gods and mortal civilizations. It could not directly act, speak or influence research. Observation was limited, not omniscience or foreknowledge. It recognized the possibility that Ascendant's unprecedented race might someday disturb the Veil, but could do nothing to cause that outcome.

The first opening for influence arose **generations later**, when exceptional Seventh researchers, unaware of the Veil or Nhal, accidentally achieved a configuration of the six fields that **resonated with** the Veil without breaching it. Only thereafter could Nhal subtly influence similar experiments, eventually steering the project intended to connect with Ascendant into the **First Anchor** and its catastrophic breach. The **First Resonance** is distinct from the First Anchor, Second Sundering and The Binding. The exact resonance physics remain open; the chronological and causal constraints are locked.
