# THEIA — HUMAN CAMPAIGN CAST, PLACES AND SERVICES

**Status: Needs Review.** Companion to the [Human Campaign](THEIA_HUMAN_CAMPAIGN.md). Existing approved characters retain their established names and offices. Every proposed destination name, new character, service assignment and future outcome here needs creator review. Appearance, exact ages, numerical abilities, succession and class rosters are not fixed by this draft.

## Geography and travel

The topology below specifies why travel is possible, not exact distances or a final world map. All allied cities are accessible socially before their formal campaign visit. Military access overseas uses existing friendly footholds on enemy land. Passage travel into the Shroud is distinct from shipborne travel between the two main continents.

If final geography makes a road or voyage implausible, move the **working site**, not a locked racial capital's terrain, the inland/coastal political distinction or the Passage rules. Exact compass bearings, travel time, coastline shape and distances require a map pass.

World geometry remains authored and static under the engine's locked architecture. “Repair a bridge/route/ward” means delivering materials, securing an existing route, enabling a service or changing a non-collision visual/object state; it does not require rebuilding terrain, destroying a building or changing a navigable door. Dungeon paths already exist in authored geometry.

## Oth'varen and the founders: what is used

Oth'varen's approved arc is Keeper of Secrets → Restorer of Trust. The Grey Surveyor is a proposed restricted occupational identity, not a replacement name or an invented bloodline. He is not a universal solution: reserves, corruption, incomplete modern knowledge and the need to protect several people at once constrain him. His six-field expertise does not allow ordinary cooperative multi-mage casting.

The five approved founders retain their names, loyalties and original specialties. [[Su'vareth]] is traced through logistics; [[Oma'vira]] threatens through a **proposed bounded prepared projection**; [[Thes'var]] contests the purpose of inversion directly; Khor'vun's defence and Vaer'uun's coordinating directives shape the finale. None dies, becomes divine, repents or is cleansed in this draft. Their ancient friendship with [[Oth'varen]] does not produce a modern reconciliation.
