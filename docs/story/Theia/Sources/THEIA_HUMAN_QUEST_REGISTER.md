# THEIA — HUMAN QUEST REGISTER

**Status: Needs Review.** Proposed quests supporting the complete [Human Campaign](THEIA_HUMAN_CAMPAIGN.md). All IDs, objectives, rewards, dependencies, dialogue and encounter mechanics are draft design. Planning bands are not tuned levels or XP commitments. This is not an imported game database or a claim of implementation.

The register contains **92 principal quests**, five solo story climaxes and **24 optional local quests**. Those counts describe this review draft, not an approved production scope. A principal quest may become several tasks or merge with a neighbour after pacing tests. Optional stories do not provide indispensable campaign evidence.

## Progression conventions

- `Start` means the campaign entry. Otherwise each row requires completion of the immediately preceding ID in its act, unless its prerequisite cell explicitly says otherwise. Cross-act prerequisites use the preceding act's final quest. These are plot dependencies only; ordinary levelling and allied-city access remain independent.
- Every critical object is an objective credit backed by a case copy. Losing an inventory item, a temporary companion or a battle cannot permanently destroy a clue. Defeat offers a checkpoint retry; story surrender/interview scenes cannot be farmed for duplicate evidence.
- Non-magical players operate tools, defend positions, inspect physical evidence and make decisions. Qualified NPCs cast specialised workings; no six-player or six-mage combined ritual is required.
- Main-story rewards are XP, suitable equipment choices and service recognition, with quantities deferred. No essential skill, economic service or faction change is awarded or withheld by a narrative choice.
- Visits to sovereign capitals require local cooperation and permission for restricted records. Arriving early never makes the city hostile or closes shops.
- Enemy-continent travel has escorted arrivals and instanced/controlled PvE approaches. A hostile player, failed territorial event or absent opponent cannot block a principal objective.

## Evidence custody and player decisions

| Decision | Quest | Consequence | Essential continuity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preserve a hazardous sample in a sealed archive, or destroy it after documentation | H2-13 | Later dialogue and an optional physical exhibit differ. | Measurements, diagrams and witness statements exist on both routes. |
| Trust Oth'varen personally or demand independent verification | H4-07 | He responds to the player's scrutiny; Sera conducts the archive comparison with them. | Independent verification always happens; trust is not a forced oath or class check. |
| Recommend severance or investigation of inversion | H5-11 | Preparation dialogue and briefing emphasis differ. | Both plans are prepared; preference remains changeable at H5-16. |
| Execute safe severance or bounded inversion | H5-16 | Local technical result and follow-up differ as described in the campaign. | Both stop the local expansion, preserve witnesses and complete the same human ending. |

For every time-sensitive-looking narrative objective, the design uses a repeatable scene or checkpoint rather than permanently deleting the only witness when the player logs out. Exact timers and encounter tuning remain production work.
