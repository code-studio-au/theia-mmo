# THEIA — HUMAN HEARTLANDS: SETTLEMENT AND NPC REGISTER

**Status:** Creator-approved settlement-services framework and recurring Heartlands cast (15 October 2026). This register distinguishes **LOCKED** service principles and scoped recurring roles from **WORKING** inventories, precise layouts and unapproved implementation. The [canonical Human campaign](Theia/Campaigns/Human_1_80_Canonical_Campaign.md) now establishes Millhaven, Stoneford, Valecrown and Greyfall Quarry; their names are not an unresolved design task. Historical suggested bands below are not independent quest gates.

**Companion sources:** [Human Act I](THEIA_HUMAN_ACT_I_STORY.md), [Zone Population Framework](THEIA_ZONE_POPULATION_FRAMEWORK.md), [Modern Story Timeline](THEIA_MODERN_STORY_REVELATION_TIMELINE.md).

## Approved design principles

The human Act I world includes a small rural starting village, an Upper Heartlands market town and the inland human racial capital, with distinct services appropriate to settlement size. Every racial starting region must provide convenient access to basic equipment, repairs, food and drink, essential class training and introductory professions. Larger towns and capitals provide broader inventories, specialist trainers, crafting stations, storage, transport and trade. Basic services must not disappear or become inaccessible because of story phasing.

Existing characters should perform useful services when their established responsibilities fit. Not every vendor requires a large character arc, but named recurring NPCs should have consistent homes, professions, motives and appearances. The settlement economy must make sense independently of the Unmade. Human local criminals, wildlife, predators and old structures provide ordinary combat.

**Current narrative identity: Millhaven.** The approved framework is a productive agricultural village with farms, a small market, militia post, smithy, inn, healing house and surrounding grazing land. **Environmental population:** Grazing animals and scavengers (neutral/attackable); local predators and dangerous scavengers (hostile); local human outlaws where the caravan theft story justifies them. The player can level, gather and explore without pursuing the royal conspiracy.

**Possible independent stories:** bridge repairs, missing livestock, farm protection, a mill dispute and minor village problems. These are working quests, not final locked quest chains.

## Settlement C — Human inland racial capital

**Current narrative identity: Valecrown.** A permanent major city, distinct from Covenant's multicultural coastal alliance capital. Available for normal travel before the formal story audience; its current story appearances follow the canonical campaign rather than the old five-act plan.

| District | Principal services | Story presence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market district | General goods, food, banking, auction house, major merchants | Merchant and shipment records |
| Artisan district | Specialised weaponsmiths, armoursmiths, crafting stations, profession trainers | Materials, repairs and equipment |
| Scholarly quarter | Magical training, archives, specialist reagents | Research and historical context |
| Military quarter | Combat trainers, military suppliers, barracks | **Garrick Ashford** and royal military offices |
| Royal quarter | Court, administration, investigative offices | **King Aldren Vaelmont**, **Mara Thorne**, **Lord Cassian Veyr** |
| Transport district | Stables, couriers and travel support; exact transport facilities remain WORKING | Connections to other Covenant territories, subject to approved travel modes and access |

The King is not a routine vendor; his audiences are major narrative events. Mara works from an investigative office rather than permanently waiting in the throne room. Garrick has military headquarters and later provides overseas mission contacts. Edric Halewyn appears through records, testimony and legacy, not as a living NPC.

**Transport supersession:** The former coach listings are **SUPERSEDED** by the later [locked Travel and Transport decision](Theia/Atlas/Travel_and_Transport.md): the initial network uses ships and fixed-route flight paths, with onward travel on foot or by mount. This service framework does not choose flight carriers, hubs, schedules, fares, route unlocks or a Stoneford–Valecrown transport service. Ordinary settlement travel support remains part of the approved framework; precise facilities remain OPEN.
